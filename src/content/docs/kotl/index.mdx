## King of the Ladder

**King of the Ladder**, also known as **KOTL**, is a competitive Minecraft minigame where players climb a ladder tower, reach the pressure plate at the top, claim the crown, and knock challengers back down.
Every successful crown claim adds to the player's total and arena score. The goal is to become king as many times as possible while keeping other players away from the top.

Built for server owners who want a continuous arena competition, **KOTL** includes an in-game setup menu, automatic area entry and exit detection, scoreboards, boss bars, king cooldowns, per-arena records, statistics, PlaceholderAPI placeholders, and a developer API for custom integrations.
