**TNT Run** can play `.nbs` songs to everyone inside an arena. Every arena has its own music, and each part of a round can use a different song — or stay silent.

<Aside type="note">
    Arena music requires the optional [NoteBlockAPI](https://github.com/xxmicloxx/NoteBlockAPI) plugin. Without it TNT Run works normally and the music menu shows a "NoteBlockAPI Not Installed" notice.
</Aside>

---

## Music Phases

A round is split into three phases. Each phase stores its own song.

| Phase | Plays when | Game states |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **Lobby** | Players are waiting for the game to start, including the countdown. | `WAITING`, `STARTING` |
| **In-Game** | The round is running and the floor is disappearing. | `IN_GAME` |
| **End-Game** | The round is over and the placements are shown. | `ENDING` |

Spectators hear the music of the current phase as well. A player who joins as a spectator in the middle of a round starts with the in-game song.

---

## Choosing Songs

<Steps>

1. Open the arena editor with `/tntrun edit <arena_id>`.
2. Click **Configure Arena Music** (`MUSIC_DISC_BLOCKS`).
3. Pick **Lobby Music**, **In-Game Music**, or **End-Game Music**.
4. In the song list:
    * `LEFT CLICK` a song to assign it.
    * `RIGHT CLICK` a song to preview it.
    * Choose **No Song** (`BARRIER`) to keep that phase silent.

</Steps>

Previews stop automatically when you close the menu, change the page, or leave the server. Changes are saved with the arena and apply from the next phase change.

---

## Continue or Restart

When two neighboring phases use the **same song**, you can decide what happens at the switch:

| Toggle | Switch | `CONTINUES` | `RESTARTS` |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| **Lobby → In-Game** (`music-game-continue-*`) | The round begins. | The song keeps playing without interruption. | The song starts again from the beginning. |
| **In-Game → End-Game** (`music-end-continue-*`) | The round ends. | The song keeps playing without interruption. | The song starts again from the beginning. |

If the songs are different, the previous song always stops and the new one starts from the beginning. The toggles are shown as a green dye (`LIME_DYE`) when the song continues and a red dye (`RED_DYE`) when it restarts. Both default to **continue**.

---

## When Music Stops

* A player leaves the arena, disconnects, or is kicked.
* The game is force-stopped by an administrator, or the server reloads or shuts down.
* The ending phase finishes and players are returned to the end location.

## Adding Songs

Songs live in `plugins/TNTRun/musics/`. Drop any `.nbs` file into the folder and restart the server; the file name (without `.nbs`) becomes the song name shown in the menu.

TNT Run copies five bundled songs into the folder on the first start only. Songs you delete are never restored, and an arena that references a missing song is reset to **No Song** at startup with a console warning.

On startup the console lists every loaded song with its length:

```text
Loaded 5 songs (14:32 of music) in 38 ms from the musics folder:
 - Bangarang [3:35]
 - Rude [3:07]
 ...
```

## Customizing the Menu

The music screens use these item keys in [`setup-menu.yml`](/tnt-run/configuration/menu/setup):

| Item Key | Purpose |
|:--|:--|
| `music-settings` | Opens the music hub from the main menu. |
| `music-lobby-selector`, `music-game-selector`, `music-end-selector` | Open the song list for one phase. |
| `music-game-continue-enabled` / `-disabled`, `music-end-continue-enabled` / `-disabled` | Continue/restart toggles for each state. |
| `song-item`, `song-no-selection` | Entries in the song list. |
| `music-border` | Glass frame around the music menus. Set `decoration-only: false` to show its tooltip or change `material`. |
| `music-previous-page`, `music-next-page`, `music-go-back` | Navigation buttons. |
