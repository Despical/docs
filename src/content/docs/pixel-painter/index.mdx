## Pixel Painter

**Pixel Painter** turns an image into a wall or floor made from Minecraft blocks. Choose its height and direction, compare material counts, then paint with perceptual color matching and optional dithering. Transparent pixels can preserve the terrain behind the image. Persistent undo journals keep original block properties available after cancellation or a server restart.

Pixel Painter supports **Paper, Paper-based forks and Folia for Minecraft 1.21.11 through 26.3** and requires **Java 25**. This guide describes **Pixel Painter 2.0.0**; older builds use different commands, sizing and undo behavior. Spigot and CraftBukkit are not supported. See [Compatibility](/pixel-painter/compatibility/) for the exact matrix.

All command permissions default to operators.

## Colors and Terrain

The bundled palette contains **305 opaque block materials**: concrete, wool, terracotta, logs, wood, ores, foliage, stable ice, mushrooms and newer waxed copper, cherry, bamboo, pale oak, resin and tuff families. Glass and blocks that change on their own after placement, such as unwaxed copper, mycelium and nylium, are never used. Oklab matching chooses representative colors; Floyd-Steinberg dithering adds detail to gradients when enabled.

Painting runs one world job at a time with a configurable block budget. The service validates build height, world borders, chunk count, journal capacity and region overlap, then saves its undo journal before placing any blocks. A painting that only crosses the world border can still be confirmed with a **[Paint anyway]** button.
