---
title: Player Command
description: Open the Item Upgrader menu for the item in the player's main hand.
---

## Open the Menu

```text
/itemupgrader
```

No built-in permission is required by default. Set `upgrade-menu-perm` in `config.yml` to require a custom permission node.

The command is player-only and checks:

- addon items have finished loading;
- the optional custom permission;
- the current world is allowed;
- the main hand is not empty;
- the material appears in `upgradable-items`;
- a recipe matches the material and display name;
- no disallowed enchantment is present;
- required ingredients and source amount are available.

The internal command fallback prefix is `iupgrader`.
