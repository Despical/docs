---
title: Arena Options
description: Reference every configurable timer, score, movement, and block option stored per The Snake arena.
---

Arena options are edited through the **Options** inventory and saved into `arenas.yml` when the editor's **Save** item is used.

## Gameplay Options

| Option | Default | Controls |
|:--|:--|:--|
| End Timer | `100` ticks | Delay between the result and teleporting the player to the end location. Left or right click changes 1 tick; shift-click changes 10. |
| Tick Interval | `6` ticks | Delay between snake movements. Left click subtracts 1 and right click adds 1. Minimum is 1. |
| Wrap Around | Enabled | Whether crossing an edge wraps to the opposite side or ends the round. |
| Head Opposite Movement | Disabled | Whether a one-block snake may reverse direction. Reversal remains blocked after growth. |
| Target Score | `0` | Points required to win. `0` means the player must collect every possible apple and fill the board. |

---

## Game Blocks

The **Customize Game Blocks** page controls:

| Option | Default |
|:--|:--|
| Snake Head | `WHITE_WOOL` |
| Snake Segments | `GRAY_WOOL` |
| Apple | `YELLOW_TERRACOTTA` |

To replace a block type, place a block item on the cursor and click the corresponding menu entry. Air and non-block items are rejected.

---

## Saving Rules

Changes remain in the active editor object until **Save** is used. Closing the menu does not replace the need to save. After all four locations are valid, saving marks the arena ready and starts its waiting task.
