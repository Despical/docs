Explore all **30 presets** in a consistent order: a distant side view, an interior view, and an overhead view. Each gallery contains three real Minecraft photographs; swipe or use the arrows to change views, and select an image to enlarge it.

Every maze uses **15 × 15 cells** and **seed 42**, with its preset's native geometry. Roofs were temporarily removed for overhead photographs and restored afterward. Roofed interior photographs use night vision so the materials remain visible.

Use the command under a preset to build the pictured style. The dimensions are cells; the physical block footprint varies with path width and wall thickness. Your editable preset files can change these values—see [preset configuration](/maze-engine/presets/) for the available settings.

**Preset:** `abyss` · **Difficulty:** Hard · **Algorithm:** Perfect

**Geometry:** paths 1 block wide; walls 4 blocks thick and 10 blocks high; roof enabled. The pictured 15 × 15 grid occupies **79 × 79 blocks**.

```text
/maze create abyss_demo 15 15 --preset abyss --seed 42
```
