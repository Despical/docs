The in-game `/maze help concepts` menu introduces the terms used by commands and chat panels. This page explains those same ideas with examples. For command syntax, see the [command reference](/maze-engine/commands/).

## Cells and Blocks

A **cell** is one position in the maze's logical grid. The width and depth supplied to `/maze create` count cells, not Minecraft blocks. Walls and paths expand that grid into the structure you see in the world.

For either horizontal axis, the block span is:

```text
cells × (path width + wall thickness) + wall thickness
```

A 15 × 15 maze with paths 3 blocks wide and walls 1 block thick occupies **61 × 61 blocks**. Increasing path width makes the same logical maze larger without adding cells. The chat panel displays both cell dimensions and the physical block dimensions.

---

## Geometry

**Path width** controls the walkable space inside each corridor. **Wall thickness** controls the barriers between corridors and around the boundary. **Wall height** controls how far those barriers rise above the floor; a preset can also add a ceiling.

Presets supply geometry defaults. Creation and preview commands can override them with `--path-width`, `--wall-thickness`, and `--wall-height`. See [generation and geometry](/maze-engine/generation/) for sizing and limits.

---

## Presets and Difficulty

A **preset** is an editable YAML theme. It combines geometry, block palettes, decorations, generation settings, and the information shown in chat. Select one with `--preset hedge`, for example, or browse the available themes with `/maze presets`.

**Easy**, **Medium**, and **Hard** are configured descriptions of the intended challenge. Changing that label alone does not reshape a maze. Saved mazes retain the settings used when they were created, so editing a preset does not silently update existing structures. See the [preset reference](/maze-engine/presets/) and [preset gallery](/maze-engine/preset-gallery/).

---

## Seeds and Complexity

A **seed** is the number that starts the generator's deterministic random sequence. Reusing the same seed, dimensions, and generation settings reproduces the same layout. Omitting `--seed` lets Maze Engine choose one; inspect the saved maze to reuse it later. Changing geometry or theme settings can change the resulting structure even when the seed stays the same.

**Complexity** is a value from `0` to `1` that adjusts generation biases. Higher values favor longer, more winding branches. It affects layout choices rather than wall height, corridor width, or the block palette. Use `--complexity 0.7`, for example, to override the preset's generation biases.

---

## Perfect and Braided Mazes

A **perfect** maze has one route between any two cells. A **braided** maze adds connections to the initial layout, creating loops and alternative routes. Both start with Maze Engine's configurable Growing Tree generator; the [generation page](/maze-engine/generation/#how-the-generator-builds-a-maze) explains its decisions and the braiding pass.

The personal **route guide** uses breadth-first search to find a shortest route from your current cell to the exit. Its arrows adapt as you move and are visible only to you. They do not modify world blocks. See [route guides](/maze-engine/previews-and-guides/#personal-route-guide).

---

## Origin and Selections

The **origin** is the structure's floor-level starting coordinate. By default, a player's maze begins 3 blocks east and 3 blocks south of their position, with its floor 1 block below their feet. Supply `--x`, `--y`, and `--z` together to choose an explicit origin. Console creation also needs `--world`.

A **WorldEdit selection** supplies a cuboid that the maze must fit inside. With `--selection`, Maze Engine calculates the largest logical grid that fits the selected area and checks the available height. Do not combine a selection with explicit dimensions or coordinate options. See [creating mazes](/maze-engine/creating-mazes/) for examples.

---

## Placement Modes

Placement controls how construction treats existing blocks:

| Mode | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| `CLEAR` | Replaces the target volume with the maze and its corridor space |
| `SAFE` | Requires air above the floor; the floor may contain air or configured replaceable blocks |
| `REPLACE` | Allows air or configured replaceable blocks throughout the volume |

These policies do not bypass region permissions or overlap checks. Players must leave the construction volume before an operation can edit it.

---

## Previews and Snapshots

A **preview** is a temporary personal display of a proposed maze. It creates neither world blocks nor a saved maze record. Once planning finishes, its **Create** button submits the retained specification for construction; **Close** removes the display. Leave the target volume before creating it. See [private previews](/maze-engine/previews-and-guides/#private-previews).

A **snapshot** preserves the original terrain, including block data, through WorldEdit or FastAsyncWorldEdit. Request it with `--snapshot` when creating a maze if you want the option to restore that terrain later. Regeneration keeps the original snapshot baseline. A snapshot is separate from the saved maze layout and is not automatically created by a preview. See [operations and recovery](/maze-engine/operations-and-recovery/) for removal and restoration policies.
