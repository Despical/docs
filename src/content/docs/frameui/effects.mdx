## Find Your Starting Point

Browse **50 animation templates and 10 ready-made presets**. Press play on any card to see its entrance, hold and exit. Search by display name or ID, or filter by category. Videos load when requested and only one plays at a time.

These are **Studio-rendered previews**, using the bundled effect definitions, renderer and sample player view. They are not gameplay recordings. Durations show the normal configured timeline; world-loading wait can extend a live effect. Verify the final design in Minecraft.

Use the command under a card to preview that ID on your server. You need `frameui.command.preview`, the accepted resource pack and eligibility under the current filters. To customize a design, [open Studio](https://frameui.despical.dev/) or use `/frameui editor` for a connected session.
