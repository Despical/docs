## FrameUI

**FrameUI** adds animated transitions after joining a server, teleporting or changing worlds. Start with **50 animation templates and 10 ready-made designs**, then customize colors, images, captions, timing and loading indicators in <a href="https://frameui.despical.dev/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">FrameUI Studio</a>.

The plugin requires **Java 25 and Paper 26.3**. Players must accept its generated resource pack. Effects start after the client world and resource pack are available; Minecraft controls its own startup and terrain-download screens.

## Configure Your Server

Each effect has its own YAML file. Global and individual filters combine to control who sees it, where it plays and how often it can run. Higher priorities win; weights choose between equally prioritized candidates.
