## About the Recordings

The recordings use the bundled template files without changes. Ten templates render as **placed heads** and ten as **floating displays** that rotate and bob; the rendering mode, scale, spin, bob, and height are set per template in [templates/](/easter-eggs/configuration/templates/). The effect shown is the template's `unfound` preset. The `collect` preset plays privately to the collecting player at the moment of discovery.

Preview any preset in your own world with `/eggs admin preview <preset>`, and tune it in [particle/](/easter-eggs/configuration/particles/). The templates are grouped by the season they were designed for, but any template can be placed in any hunt.

The [menu guide](/easter-eggs/menus/) shows the player screens and administrator editors.
