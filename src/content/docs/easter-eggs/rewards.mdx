Rewards are defined in [rewards.yml](/easter-eggs/configuration/rewards/) as **groups** of **rules**. Hunts list the groups they use, and templates can add extra groups for eggs of that template. This page explains what happens from the moment a reward is earned until it reaches the player.

---

## Where Rules Apply

```yaml
# hunts/easter.yml
rewards:
  - 'common'
  - 'seasonal-results'
  - 'spring-trail'

# templates/golden-egg.yml
rewards:
  - 'special-egg'
```

A discovery is checked against every rule in the hunt's groups **plus** the groups of the collected egg's template. A rule can also be limited to one template with `template: <template-id>`. Unknown group IDs are rejected when the configuration loads.

---

## Triggers

| Trigger | Earned when | Earned at most |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `EGG_FOUND` | A placement is discovered for the first time in the period | Once per egg per period |
| `FIRST_FIND` | The player's first discovery in the hunt's period | Once per period |
| `COUNT` | The discovery count reaches exactly `threshold` | Once per period |
| `PERCENT` | Progress crosses `threshold` percent (1 to 100) of the eggs currently placed | Once per period |
| `EVERY` | The discovery count is a multiple of `threshold` | Once per multiple per period |
| `TYPE_COMPLETE` | Every placement of the discovered egg's template in the hunt is found | Once per template per period |
| `HUNT_COMPLETE` | The hunt is completed for the first time in the period | Once per period |
| `PLACEMENT` | The completion is the `threshold`-th completion of the hunt in this period (1 = first finisher) | Once per period |
| `PERSONAL_BEST` | A timed completion sets the player's first time or beats their best time | Once per distinct time |
| `SERVER_BEST` | A timed completion sets the hunt's first time or beats the server's best time | Once per distinct time |
| `EVENT_RANK` | The player finishes at rank `threshold` when a scheduled hunt ends | Once per season end |

The final egg can trigger both its discovery rules and the completion rules. `PERSONAL_BEST` and `SERVER_BEST` only apply to timed hunts and only to genuine improvements; an imported or unknown time of zero never counts. `EVENT_RANK` only fires for hunts with a [schedule end](/easter-eggs/hunt-rules/#season-rankings).

`COUNT`, `PERCENT`, and `EVERY` are evaluated only on first-time discoveries, so re-collecting eggs during a retry cannot reach a milestone twice.

## Durable Claims

When a rule matches, the plugin records a **claim** in the same database transaction as the discovery. The claim is keyed by player, hunt, reward period, and a stable rule identity (`group.rule`, plus the egg, template, interval, or time where relevant). A claim that already exists can never be earned again in that period, so rewards are not repeated by:

- reloads, restarts, or relogs;
- timed retries in the same period;
- moving an egg, because its identity is kept;
- removing and re-adding the same rule, as long as its group and rule IDs are unchanged.

<Aside type="caution">
Renaming a group or rule creates a **new** reward identity, which players can earn again. Keep IDs stable once a hunt is live; disable a rule with `enabled: false` instead of renaming it.
</Aside>

A new [reward period](/easter-eggs/hunt-rules/#reward-periods) renews every claim.

### Weighted Pools

A rule with `random-enabled: true` adds **exactly one** option from its `pool` to its fixed `actions` each time the claim is earned. Weights are relative: an option's chance is its weight divided by the sum of all weights. The draw happens once, when the claim is created, so the outcome cannot be rerolled by reconnecting. The reward menu shows every option with its chance.

---

## Delivery

Earning a reward and delivering it are separate steps:

1. The claim and its actions are written to a durable queue together with the discovery.
2. While the player is online and loaded, queued actions are delivered in order, about twice per second.
3. Each action is reserved, then executed, then marked `DONE`.

Actions earned by an offline player, such as season ranking rewards, wait in the queue until they join. Placeholder values such as `%found%` are captured when the reward is earned, so later progress does not change them.

Plugins can veto an individual action with [`RewardDeliverEvent`](/easter-eggs/api/events/#reward-events). A vetoed action is marked `CANCELLED`; it is not retried and the claim stays earned.

### Uncertain Deliveries

External effects such as commands, economy deposits, and inventory changes cannot be rolled back with the database. If an action fails, for example a command that is not handled or a missing economy provider, or if the server stops while an action is in progress, the action is marked `UNCERTAIN` instead of being retried automatically. This prevents double payouts after a crash.

Startup logs how many interrupted operations need inspection. Review them with `/eggs admin deliveries` and resolve each one; see [Operations and Recovery](/easter-eggs/operations/#uncertain-deliveries).

## Reward Inbox

Only `ITEM` rewards use the inbox. When an item does not fit in the player's inventory, the remaining stacks are stored in the database instead of being dropped, and the player receives **Rewards Saved**.

- Open it with **Unclaimed rewards** in the hunt list or `/eggs inbox`. The button only appears while items are waiting.
- Click an item to move it to your inventory. Items that still do not fit stay in the inbox.
- Online players with waiting items are reminded every `pending.reminder-seconds`.
- Items expire after `pending.expire-after-seconds` (default three hours, `0` for never). Within the last `pending.warning-seconds`, reminders become **Rewards Expiring**. Expired items are deleted, also while the player is offline, and the player is told how many expired.

Commands, money, XP, and cosmetic actions never enter the inbox. Changing the expiry setting only affects newly stored items.
