<EasterEggsGuide>

Enable the desired board in `config.yml` under `leaderboard.boards`. Replace rank with a one-based position up to the configured limit, and hunt with a configured hunt ID.

| Board | Example name placeholder |
| --- | --- |
| Global discoveries | %eastereggs_leaderboard:global:1:name% |
| Hunt discoveries | %eastereggs_leaderboard:hunt:easter:1:name% |
| Current reward period | %eastereggs_leaderboard:cycle:easter:1:name% |
| Fastest timed completion | %eastereggs_leaderboard:fastest:easter:1:name% |

Replace the final `name` with `value` or `uuid` to obtain the score/duration or player UUID. Discovery values are counts; fastest values are raw milliseconds and lower is better.

FASTEST is available only for timed hunts with positive completion durations. CYCLE is omitted for player-relative COOLDOWN hunts. Disabled boards and positions without a result have no published entry. Rankings refresh asynchronously and may lag a recent completion.

</EasterEggsGuide>
