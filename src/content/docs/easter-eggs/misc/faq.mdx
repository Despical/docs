## Which servers are supported?

Use Paper or a compatible fork for Minecraft 1.21.11 or later, with Java 25. 3.0.0 does not support Folia or the old 1.8-to-modern Spigot range. See [installation](/easter-eggs/getting-started/).

## Why are there no eggs after installation?

Bundled files define hunts, templates and effects; they do not create world placements. Use `/eggs admin` or `/eggs admin give easter painted-egg`, then place the head.

## Why can I place eggs but not collect them?

Leave placement mode with `/eggs admin edit off`. Then check the collect permission, hunt availability, entry requirements, ordering, shared claims and whether a timed attempt needs `/eggs start <hunt>`.

## Why are particles or boss bars missing?

Check player preferences, the global particle budget and view distance, the template's state preset and the hunt's boss-bar setting. Boss bars and hot/cold feedback are off in the bundled hunts until enabled.

## Why is my inbox empty?

Only item rewards that could not fit become pending items. The inbox command is still available with permission, but empty inbox menu shortcuts are hidden. Items may also have passed their configured expiry deadline.

## Why did a retry or reset not award prizes again?

Attempt state and reward rights are separate. Claimed rewards remain claimed within the same reward period. Configure a repeat policy for renewed rewards.

## Why do money rewards fail?

MONEY requires Vault and a working economy provider. Installing Vault alone does not create an economy. Check the provider and [uncertain deliveries](/easter-eggs/operations/#uncertain-deliveries) before retrying a payment.

## Where is mysql.yml?

3.0.0 uses `storage.mysql` inside config.yml. Switching backends does not copy existing data. See [database setup](/easter-eggs/configuration/mysql/).

## Can I keep my 2.x data?

Use the explicit legacy importer from the console with no players online. Review its report and disabled imported hunts before enabling them. See [migration](/easter-eggs/operations/).

## What happens when a reload fails?

The current running configuration remains active. Correct the reported file, value or reference, then reload again. Storage changes need a restart.
