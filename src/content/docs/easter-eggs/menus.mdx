## Player Menus

| Screen | How to open it |
| --- | --- |
| **Egg Hunts** | `/eggs` |
| **Hunt page** | Choose a hunt, or `/eggs menu <hunt>` |
| **Egg Collection** | **Your Progress** on a hunt page |
| **Hunt Rewards** | **Rewards** on a hunt page, or `/eggs rewards <hunt>` |
| **Leaderboards** | **Leaderboard** in the hunt list, or `/eggs leaderboard [hunt]` |
| **Preferences** | **Preferences** in the hunt list, or `/eggs preferences` |
| **Reward Inbox** | **Unclaimed rewards** in the hunt list (shown only when items are waiting), or `/eggs inbox` |

### Hunts and Progress

The hunt list shows every hunt in `menu-order` with its mode, availability, found count, and a progress bar. A hunt page shows your progress, elapsed or remaining time, and the actions that apply:

- **Untimed hunts** show progress, **Get a Hint**, **Rewards**, and **Egg Collection**. There is nothing to start or leave.
- **Timed hunts** add **Start Hunt** (start or retry) and **Leave Attempt**, which asks for confirmation.

The collection lists every egg with its order number, marked **Found** or **Still Hidden**. It never reveals where an unfound egg is.

### Rewards

The reward gallery lists every rule from the hunt's reward groups and from the templates placed in it. Each card shows the trigger, what it grants, whether you already earned it in the current period, and your remaining progress toward it. Weighted pools show each option with its chance; pools with no fixed actions appear as a mystery Ender Chest.

<ScreenshotGallery label="Reward galleries" images={shots(["rewards", "rewards-easter", "rewards-page-2"])} />

### Leaderboards

Choose a ranking, then a hunt where needed:

| Ranking | Ranks by | Config value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Server Total** | All-time discoveries across every hunt | `GLOBAL` |
| **By Hunt** | All-time discoveries in one hunt | `HUNT` |
| **Since Last Reset** | Discoveries in the hunt's current reward period | `CYCLE` |
| **Fastest Finish** | Best completion time in a timed hunt | `FASTEST` |

Only rankings listed in `leaderboard.boards` are offered, with up to `leaderboard.limit` rows.

<ScreenshotGallery label="Leaderboard screens" images={shots(["leaderboard", "board-hunts", "board-results"])} />

### Reward Inbox

Item rewards that do not fit in your inventory are saved here. Click an item to collect it; anything that still does not fit stays in the inbox. Items expire after `pending.expire-after-seconds`. See [Rewards](/easter-eggs/rewards/#reward-inbox).

## Hunt Editor

`/eggs admin` opens the **Hunt Editor** with every hunt and a **Create Hunt** button. Choosing a hunt opens its **Hunt Dashboard**:

| Button | Opens |
| --- | --- |
| **Add Eggs** | **Egg Templates**: click one to receive a placement head |
| **Placed Eggs** | Every placement with world, position, order, template, effects, and clue source |
| **Egg Hints** | Placements in collection order; choose one to type its clue |
| **Collection Order** | Placements in collection order; choose one to type its order number |
| **Hunt Rules** | Open Hunt, Require Order, Who Can Collect?, Reward Period, and **Time Settings** |
| **Hints & Displays** | Text Hints, Hot & Cold, Progress Bar, Hide Found Eggs, World Protection |
| **Reset & Removal** | Reset Attempts and Delete Hunt, each with a confirmation screen |

Clicking a placed egg opens **Egg Tools** with Move, Duplicate, Edit Hint, Collection Order, and Remove. Drop an item onto the dashboard's hunt card to make it the hunt icon.

Every button checks the permission of the matching command, for example **Remove Egg** requires `eastereggs.admin.remove`. Settings changes are validated and applied immediately, and success or failure is confirmed with a sound. Text prompts are typed in chat and offer a **[Cancel]** button; they expire after a short time or when you log out.
