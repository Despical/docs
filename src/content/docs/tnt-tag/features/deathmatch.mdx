---
title: Deathmatch
description: Configure TNT Tag's final phase and distinguish it from the one-tagger threshold.
---

Deathmatch begins when the number of active players is less than or equal to `Deathmatch-Settings.After`. It is designed to keep the final rounds focused and prevent players from remaining spread across a large map.

When `Deathmatch-Settings.TP-To-Spawn-Point` is enabled, alive players return to the arena start location as new deathmatch rounds begin. Disable it if your map is small or if forced teleports interfere with the intended layout.

## Separate Thresholds

Deathmatch and tagger count use different settings:

| Setting | Effect |
|:--|:--|
| `Deathmatch-Settings.After` | Enables the final match phase. |
| `Round-Settings.Only-One-Tagged-After` | Forces a single tagged player per round. |

The two values may be equal, but they do not have to be. Test the transition with the smallest realistic player group before selecting production values.
