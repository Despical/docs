---
title: Join Signs
description: Create TNT Tag arena join signs and customize their live text.
---

Join signs provide a clickable arena entry point and display live arena information.

## Create a Sign

1. Open `/tnttag edit <arena>`.
2. Select the sign setup option.
3. Look directly at the sign block you want to register.
4. Confirm the selection and save the arena.

The sign location is stored under the arena in `arena.yml`. Breaking a registered sign removes its association when the player has the required permission.

## Display Tokens

The `signs` section of `messages.yml` supports `%state%`, `%map_name%`, `%players%`, and `%max_players%`. State labels come from `arena-states` in the same file.

## Permissions

TNT Tag 1.9.5 checks `tnttag.admin.sign.create` and `tnttag.admin.sign.break` at runtime. See [Admin Commands](/tnt-tag/commands/admin#join-sign-permissions) for the declared-permission mismatch in this version.
