---
title: Spectator Mode
description: Configure TNT Tag eliminated players, late joins, spectator settings, visibility, and teleport tools.
---

Eliminated players remain inside the match as spectators. They can use the configured settings item and teleporter compass without affecting active players.

## Spectator Preferences

The settings menu controls spectator speed, night vision, and whether other spectators are shown or hidden. Supported speed choices range from no effect through levels I to IV. These preferences are stored with the player's spectator-related data.

## Teleporter

The teleporter menu lists active players. Selecting a player moves the spectator to that player's location. `TP-Spawn-After-Exploding` separately controls the initial teleport immediately after elimination.

## Late Joins

A player who joins during `IN_GAME` or `ENDING` becomes a permanent spectator. Permanent spectators can watch, but they are not inserted into the alive-player pool and do not receive ordinary end-game statistics or rewards.

## Visibility

Spectators are hidden from active players. `Hide-Players` additionally controls whether players inside the arena are hidden from people outside it.
